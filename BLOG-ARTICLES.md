# NodeFlux Blog: First 3 Articles (Draft Plan)

A blog section that targets **what Singapore SMEs actually type into Google**. Each article is written to rank for local intent, build trust, and funnel readers into a "Get in touch" or "Free Website Audit" CTA.

The goal is not to write generic web-dev content. It's to answer the questions Singapore business owners are *already* asking, before they pick a vendor.

---

## SEO & Audience Strategy

**Target audience:** Singapore SME owners, founders, F&B operators, retailers, professional service firms (clinics, law firms, consultants) with revenue between S$200K and S$5M who need a website, web app, or mobile presence but don't know where to start.

**Tone:** Direct, honest, no jargon. The same voice Abhay uses on the homepage ("I actually pick up the phone"). No fluff, no "in today's digital world" intros.

**SEO essentials per article:**
- One primary long-tail keyword in the H1 and slug
- 1,200 to 2,000 words (enough depth to rank, not so long readers bail)
- Singapore-specific context: PSG grant, IMDA, local pricing in SGD, GST, .com.sg domains
- Internal links to `/services`, `/contact`, and `/services` pricing tiers
- A concrete CTA at the end: Free Website Audit or "Talk to Abhay"
- Schema.org `Article` JSON-LD

## Article 1: "How Much Does a Website Cost in Singapore in 2026? A No-BS Pricing Breakdown for SMEs"

**Slug:** `/blog/website-cost-singapore-2026`
**Primary keyword:** `how much does a website cost in Singapore`
**Secondary:** `website price Singapore`, `web design cost SME Singapore`, `PSG grant website`
**Target length:** ~1,600 words
**Target reading time:** 8 min

### Why this article
This is the #1 search Singapore SMEs make before reaching out to any vendor. Most existing results are either vendor sales pages with no real numbers, or generic global articles that quote USD. There's a genuine content gap for an honest, SGD-priced breakdown written by someone who builds these every week.

### Outline
1. **TL;DR pricing table** (template S$700 to 1.5K, custom S$1.5K to 5K, web app S$5K to 15K, enterprise S$15K+)
2. **What's actually inside that quote**: design, dev, copy, hosting, domain, SSL, who pays for what
3. **The hidden costs nobody mentions**: content writing, photography, maintenance retainers, plugin licences, future re-platforming
4. **PSG Grant: how it actually works in 2026**: eligibility, the 50% subsidy, pre-approved vendors, common rejection reasons
5. **Template vs. custom vs. agency**: honest tradeoffs, when each makes sense
6. **5 red flags when getting quotes** (no written scope, hosting lock-in, no source code handover, vague timeline, no post-launch support)
7. **What S$700, S$2K, S$5K and S$10K actually buy you**: concrete examples
8. **CTA:** Free Website Audit + link to pricing page

## Article 2: "Do I Need a Mobile App for My Business? An Honest Guide for Singapore SMEs"

**Slug:** `/blog/do-i-need-a-mobile-app-singapore`
**Primary keyword:** `do I need a mobile app for my business`
**Secondary:** `mobile app vs website Singapore`, `app development cost Singapore SME`, `PWA vs native app`
**Target length:** ~1,400 words
**Target reading time:** 7 min

### Why this article
Most SMEs assume "we need an app" because a competitor has one, then spend S$30K+ on something that gets 12 downloads. This article saves the reader from that mistake, which is exactly the kind of honesty that converts.

### Outline
1. **The short answer:** probably not, and here's how to know for sure
2. **The 4 questions that decide it:**
   - Do users open you weekly+? (Loyalty, booking, ordering = yes. One-off services = no.)
   - Do you need push notifications, camera, GPS, or offline?
   - Do you have a budget for ongoing iOS + Android maintenance (S$300 to 800/month)?
   - Is your website experience already broken on mobile?
3. **The middle ground: Progressive Web Apps (PWA)**: installable, no App Store, 1/3 the cost
4. **Real Singapore examples**: when an F&B QR-ordering web app beats a native app, when a clinic booking app makes sense
5. **What a native app actually costs in Singapore** (S$8K to 25K MVP, plus annual maintenance)
6. **App Store + Play Store costs and approval timelines** in 2026
7. **A simple decision tree** (graphic / table)
8. **CTA:** Free 20-min consult to figure out which path fits

## Article 3: "PSG Grant for Websites & Digital Solutions in Singapore (2026): Eligibility, Process, and What to Watch For"

**Slug:** `/blog/psg-grant-website-singapore-2026`
**Primary keyword:** `PSG grant website Singapore`
**Secondary:** `productivity solutions grant 2026`, `IMDA pre-approved vendor website`, `how to apply PSG grant`
**Target length:** ~1,500 words
**Target reading time:** 7 min

### Why this article
"PSG grant website" is a high-intent search. Anyone typing it is ready to spend money. Ranking here puts NodeFlux in front of pre-qualified buyers. Most existing articles are outdated (2022/2023 figures) or written by grant consultancies pushing their service.

### Outline
1. **What PSG actually covers in 2026**: current support level, capped amounts, eligible solution categories
2. **Are you eligible? The 4 criteria** (registered & operating in SG, ≥30% local shareholding, group revenue ≤S$100M or ≤200 employees, not previously subsidised for the same solution)
3. **Step-by-step: from "I want this" to grant disbursed** (timeline, BGP portal, CorpPass, supporting docs)
4. **What's "pre-approved" and why it matters**: only IMDA-listed solutions qualify
5. **Common rejection reasons** (incomplete quote, wrong vendor, missing financials)
6. **The catch nobody tells you**: you pay full upfront, claim back later; cashflow planning
7. **Realistic timeline:** 6 to 10 weeks for approval + disbursement
8. **What NodeFlux can and can't help with** (honest scope: we build the website; we point you to grant consultants for the paperwork)
